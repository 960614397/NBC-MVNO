SECTION 01

What is an MVNO?

A Mobile Virtual Network Operator (MVNO) is a brand that sells mobile services without owning the underlying radio network. It rides on a host MNO's coverage, paying wholesale rates for spectrum and network access — while owning the customer relationship, brand, and service layer entirely.

MVNOs win on value
The network is a commodity. The experience, proposition, and ecosystem are the competitive edge. For NBC, the mobile SIM becomes a daily banking touchpoint that no pure telco can replicate.
01
Non-comparative proposition
Bundle connectivity into your existing banking brand — a price that can't be apples-to-apples compared with an MNO.
02
Greater customer entanglement
Mobile services touch every day of your customer's life. Loyalty becomes structural, not promotional.
03
First-party behavioural data
Passively collected usage data feeds personalisation, retention and new banking revenue lines.
04
New high-margin revenue
Layered VAS — fintech, security, content, insurance — unlock margins that pure connectivity cannot.
40%+
MVNO Nation CX Study
Average MVNO churn without strong CX. The MVNOs that win don't compete on price — they win on relevance. For NBC, banking relationships are already built. The MVNO deepens them.

NBC Tanzania Use Cases

Ten differentiated use cases.
Zero that a pure telco can replicate.

Each use case is grounded in NBC's existing banking infrastructure — built on relationships, data, and trust that took decades to build. The MVNO layer activates all of it.

NBC Banking SIM — Access & Inclusion Layer

A branded SIM/eSIM enabling always-on, frictionless access to NBC banking. Zero-rated mobile banking (USSD + app). Data and airtime rewards accumulate with every account transaction. Simplified digital onboarding removes the branch as a dependency.

✓ Financial inclusion at scale · Higher digital banking adoption · Reduced cost-to-serve
⚡

Transaction Acceleration Engine

NBC already processes transactions. The MVNO drives them — at scale, with intent. Data rewards triggered automatically on every qualifying deposit, transfer, and payment. Streak mechanics reward consistent digital banking month on month.

✓ Measurable uplift in transaction volumes · Higher deposit frequency · Deeper engagement
🏪

SME & Merchant Growth Platform

Business SIM plans, POS and payment connectivity, real-time transaction alerts and zero-rated access to NBC business tools. NBC stops being just a bank and becomes an active partner in business growth for SMEs, merchants, and informal traders.

✓ NBC as business growth enabler · Higher SME wallet share · New recurring revenue per merchant
🤝

Agent Banking Enablement Layer

Dedicated, priority SIM connectivity ensuring agent uptime in low-signal areas. Real-time transaction processing, liquidity and float monitoring, and a direct communication channel to NBC HQ. Every agent becomes a data-generating endpoint.

✓ Improved agent uptime · Expanded rural reach · Real-time network intelligence
💳

Salary & Employee Banking Integration

Monthly data and airtime automatically linked to confirmed salary deposits. Tiered rewards scaling with tenure and product depth. Employee financial wellbeing tools zero-rated on the NBC SIM. An employer partnership framework for corporates.

✓ Increased salary account stickiness · Higher cross-sell · Reduced primary banking attrition
📊

Lending & Repayment Behaviour Platform

Automated data rewards on confirmed on-time loan repayments. Milestone incentives at 3, 6, and 12-month repayment marks. Behaviour-linked signals augment NBC's credit scoring models. Early-warning engagement triggers for at-risk customers.

✓ Improved repayment rates · Reduced default provisioning · Enhanced credit scoring data
🌾

Agriculture & Rural Banking Enablement

Reliable connectivity for smallholder farmers and cooperatives. Market price updates and weather alerts via USSD and app. Zero-rated NBC agri-lending and insurance tools. Savings incentives for Tanzania's largest economic sector.

✓ Increased rural financial inclusion · Growth in agri-finance portfolio
🌍

Cross-Border & Trade Connectivity

Affordable roaming solutions for customers transacting across Tanzania's borders. Incentives for cross-border payments processed through NBC. Integration with regional EAC and SADC trade corridors. Dual eSIM for seamless cross-border banking.

✓ NBC as the bank of regional commerce · Increased cross-border transaction volumes
👨‍🎓

Youth & Digital Banking Platform

Data bundles linked directly to youth account activity and savings milestones. Rewards and gamification mechanics for digital banking engagement. Zero-rated access to financial education and student resources. Social referral mechanics.

✓ Early acquisition in highest-growth demographic · NBC as the default bank for digital-native Tanzania
⭐

Loyalty & Retention Engine

Monthly data and airtime rewards automatically linked to active account status. Tiered loyalty — Bronze, Silver, Gold — scaled to banking tenure and product depth. Churn prediction signals surfaced in real time via Xanite CVM for proactive intervention.

✓ Measurably lower churn · Increased customer lifetime value · NBC as the bank customers choose to stay with
