Agent Banking Enablement Layer

Dedicated connectivity platform for NBC's agent network. Reliable SIM connectivity, real-time transaction processing, liquidity and float monitoring.

✓ Improved agent uptime · Expanded rural reach

Cross-Border & Remittance Connectivity

Seamless connectivity for customers operating across borders. Affordable local data rates, cross-border SIM usage, and incentives for cross-border payments.

✓ NBC strengthens role in regional commerce and trade finance

Youth & Digital Banking Platform

Digital-first connectivity targeting younger, mobile-first customers. Data bundles linked to youth accounts, rewards for digital banking, zero-rated access to education and financial tools.

Agriculture & Rural Banking Enablement
